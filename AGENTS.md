## What Shiny Computers Homebrew Tap does

This Homebrew tap distributes Shiny Computers formulae and casks.
Its documented bd-to-avp formula installs the Apple Silicon command-line converter; the desktop app is distributed separately.
Evidence: [README.md](README.md#available-formulae).
